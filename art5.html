The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Artist: Katsushika Hokusai

Year: c. 1831

Category: Ukiyo-e

The Great Wave off Kanagawa is a famous Japanese woodblock print by Katsushika Hokusai. It shows a giant wave towering over small boats, with Mount Fuji visible in the background.
