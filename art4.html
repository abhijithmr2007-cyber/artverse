Girl with a Pearl Earring

Artist: Johannes Vermeer

Year: c. 1665

Category: Dutch Golden Age

Girl with a Pearl Earring is a famous painting by Johannes Vermeer. It shows a young woman wearing a blue and yellow turban and a large pearl earring. The painting is admired for its lighting and realistic appearance.
